# How to log in to most of all of Phoenix Limited's services
Most services are "SSO" enabled, meaning you can log in to the single sign on portal, and have access to all the apps. 

##### From the app
:::warning
The main username/email & password boxes won't typically work, showing an invalid user or password error. Thats because one needs to use the SSO Portal. Too many failed login attempts my temporarily block your IP, typically giving an error 1033.
:::

Most sites will have a "Log in with SSO" or "Log in the Phoenix Limited SSO" button. Use this to successfully log in
