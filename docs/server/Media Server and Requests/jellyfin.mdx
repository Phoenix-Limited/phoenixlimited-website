# Logging into Jellyfin

Choose the system that you're trying to log into below

:::note
If you're encountering issues with logging in, check [the uptime page for the media server](https://uptime.phoenixlimited.net/status/media) before anything else. Then make sure you can log into [the SSO portal](https://auth.phoenixlimited.net).
:::

:::info
Most of the different website run **independently of each other**. This means while you may be able to get to the SSO portal, you may not be able to get to Jellyfin or vise versa. If you are already logged in and Jellyfin is working, the SSO portal doesn't necessarily need to work to enjoy Jellyfin content.
:::

  <TabItem value="web" label="Website" default>
  	  <Tabs>
		 <TabItem value="sso" label="SSO Portal" default>
			1. From the main screen [on the SSO portal](https://auth.phoenixlimited.net), click the ```Jellyfin Media Server``` icon
		</TabItem>
		 <TabItem value="direct" label="Sign in with Phoenix Limited SSO">
			1. Open the [website](https://media.phoenixlimited.net)
			2. Click on **Sign in with Phoenix Limited SSO**
			3. Enter your login credentials and select Log in
			:::info
			Here, you can select stay signed in to remember your log in credentials for 30 days
			:::
		</TabItem>
		  <TabItem value="quickconnect" label="Quick connect with another logged in device" default>
		    1. Log into Jellyfin **via the Website method**, or open any other already logged in app
		    2. Click the Profile Icon in the top right corner
		    3. Click quick connect and have this screen ready
		    4. Open the [website](https://media.phoenixlimited.net)
		    5. Click **Use Quick Connect**
		    6. Take the 6 digit code proved and type it in your other device and click **Authorize**
		    :::note
			You will stay logged in after this
			:::
		 </TabItem>
	</Tabs>
  </TabItem>
